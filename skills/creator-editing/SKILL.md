# Creator editing workflow

Read ../../README.md, ../../docs/agent-workflow.md and ../../docs/status.md.
Use the documented CLI; do not modify engine source to accomplish an edit.

1. Discover project/brief; inspect metadata, timeline, settings and transcript.
2. Analyze source contact sheets and relevant timeline windows. Correct ASR
   errors before deriving boundaries. Obtain consent for external uploads.
3. Interpret the creative brief and content profile; propose a coherent story,
   required/excluded moments and target formats. Explain uncertainty.
4. Author a versioned plan using inspect's revision, sentence/word timestamps
   and reasoned selections. Never include executable instructions.
5. Validate, resolve errors, then apply. Preview the new revision.
6. Read technical QA. Inspect rendered cut boundaries and caption readability.
   Creative review must assess context, hook, repetition, payoff and pacing;
   label these judgments subjective. Do not call incomplete QA fully verified.
7. Iterate with structured patches/plans and preserve history. After creator
   approval, approve/export the exact revision for requested platforms.
8. Record accepted/rejected choices with feedback and explain output paths.

For music without transcript punctuation use manual range patches rather than
pretending the speech planner understands musical structure. For missing engine
features use supported legacy helpers explicitly and disclose the separate output.
